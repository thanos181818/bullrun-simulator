# BullRun - Trading Simulator

Master the market risk-free with our realistic trading simulator! Practice trading stocks and crypto with virtual money and build your confidence before putting real funds on the line.

---

## Key Features

### Simulated Trading
- Practice buying and selling stocks & crypto with virtual cash
- Real-time market prices for authentic experience
- Track your portfolio performance over time

### Portfolio Management
- View your holdings, returns, and transaction history
- Interactive charts to visualize your progress
- Daily bonus system to keep you engaged

### Learning & Gamification
- Earn badges for trading achievements
- AI-powered insights and market analysis
- Watchlist feature to track your favorite assets
- Rule Book with guides and tutorials

### Global Support
- Multiple language support (English, Spanish, French, German, Chinese)
- Dark/light/system theme options
- Responsive design for all devices

## How to Get Started

1. Sign up or log in - Create your free account
